PapirGlider Klubben

A Norwegian workshop for precise folds, long glides and the small joy of an A4 sheet suddenly behaving like an aircraft.

Surface

Type
Web
Stack
Web, HTML/CSS
Role
Design and development

Open PapirGlider Klubben · Docker version

Challenge

PapirGlider needed a workshop, not an empty welcome page: a place where you can fold with your hands while learning why the plane climbs, rolls or dives.

Move

We built a calm entry into three core models — classic dart, Nakamura lock and a broad glider — with guides, trim and throwing advice, plus selected international videos embedded on the site.

Stack

A light static surface with clear typography and imagery that carries the club’s character. The content stays concrete: centre of gravity, wing angle, symmetry and throwing technique.

Result

PapirGlider Klubben is live at laden.no/levi. It invites people to fold neatly, throw calmly and adjust one thing at a time — pocket-sized aerodynamics without losing the play.
